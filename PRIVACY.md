# AI Benar privacy policy

**Last updated:** November 2026

AI Benar runs **on your own device**. It does not collect, sell or send the pages you audit, your results, your readiness plan, your browsing history or any personal information to anyone. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers or external servers.

## What AI Benar reads
Only when you ask it to audit a page (by clicking the button, using a shortcut or the right-click menu), AI Benar reads what any visitor’s browser already receives from that page: its content, the files it has loaded, its readable cookies, its own response headers and its public `/.well-known/security.txt` file. To check the headers it re-requests the page once, and for the secrets check it fetches a few of the site’s own scripts — all of these requests go to the site being audited, and nothing is sent anywhere else. Audit results can include the page’s public email addresses and form field names. It does not submit forms, probe servers or test for weaknesses.

## What is stored, and where
- **Audit results** (the last 25) are stored in your browser’s local extension storage.
- **Your chosen laws** are stored in Chrome sync storage so they follow you between your own browsers.
- **Your readiness navigator answers** are stored in local extension storage.
You can delete audits in *History* and clear your answers with *Start again*.

## Permissions
- **activeTab, scripting:** read the page you choose to audit.
- **cookies:** list the cookies that page sets, to check consent and security settings.
- **browsingData:** only for *Re-test as a new visitor*, to clear that one site’s cookies and storage in your browser before reloading it.
- **Optional site access:** requested for one site at a time when you use *Re-test as a new visitor*.
- **storage, contextMenus:** save your settings and add AI Benar to the right-click menu.

## Not legal advice
Results are indicative and based on passive checks. Check the official text of each law, or ask a qualified adviser, before making important decisions.
