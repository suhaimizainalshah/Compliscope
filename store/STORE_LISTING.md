# Chrome Web Store listing for AI Benar

## Name
AI Benar: Privacy, AI Law and Security Checker

## Summary (132 characters max)
Check websites for GDPR, EU AI Act and Asian and US AI and data laws, with plain fixes, an AI law atlas and a plan for non-profits.

## Description
AI Benar checks what a website does in your browser and explains, in plain language, what may break privacy, AI and security rules, and how to fix it.

PRIVACY AND COOKIES
• Tracking that starts before visitors agree, consent banners without a Reject button, pre-ticked boxes
• Missing privacy notices, personal data leaking to third parties, data sent abroad
• Sensitive data in forms, marketing boxes ticked by default, tracking on sites for children

EU AI ACT
• AI chatbots that don’t say they’re AI (Article 50)
• Possible high-risk uses in education, employment and essential services
• Possible banned uses, such as emotion recognition of students or staff

SECURITY BASICS
• HTTPS, security headers, cookie settings, outdated libraries with known vulnerabilities
• Secret keys accidentally published in page code (shown masked)

THE LAWS THAT APPLY
• EU GDPR and EU AI Act by default, plus UK, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, India, Pakistan, China, South Korea, Australia, the US, Brazil, South Africa, Kenya and Nigeria
• Every finding shows the matching sections for each law you choose

AI AND DATA SECURITY ATLAS
• Dated profiles of AI and data rules in ASEAN, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, India, Pakistan, China, South Korea and the United States
• What’s binding, voluntary or in draft, breach deadlines, DPO rules and what it means for your website

READINESS NAVIGATOR
• A plain-language plan for schools, charities, NGOs and non-profits across countries, with AI Act classification, an action plan and ready-to-use templates

Passive checks only, all on your device. Not legal advice.

## Category
Productivity (or Developer Tools)

## Single purpose
Check websites for privacy, AI transparency and basic security issues, and help organisations plan compliance.

## Permission justifications
- activeTab, scripting: read the page the user chooses to audit.
- cookies: list cookies set by the audited page to check consent and security settings.
- browsingData: clear one site’s cookies and storage, only when the user chooses “Re-test as a new visitor”.
- Optional host access: requested for one site at a time for re-testing.
- storage: save results, chosen laws and the user’s plan on the device.
- contextMenus: add “Audit this site” to the right-click menu.

## Data usage disclosure
Does not collect or transmit any user data.
